# SuperStore Sales Forecast Dashboard

## 1. Project Title / Headline

📈 **SuperStore Sales Forecast – 15 Days Predictive Analytics Dashboard**

A performance-driven, interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze historical sales trends and forecast the next 15 days for smarter business decision-making across U.S. states.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The SuperStore Sales Forecast Dashboard is a visually rich Power BI report built to help users analyze historical sales performance and generate 15-day predictive forecasts using time-series analysis. It assists sales analysts, managers, and business stakeholders in identifying demand patterns, high-performing states, and actionable insights to optimize planning and profitability.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

* 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Main visualization & reporting tool
* 📂 **Power Query** – Data cleaning, transformation, and preparation
* 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Measures, custom logic, and aggregation
* 📝 **Data Modeling** – Relationship design enabling filtering across visuals
* 🔮 **Built-in Power BI Forecasting** – Time-series predictive modeling
* 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews

---

## 4. Data Source

**Dataset:** Global SuperStore Sales Data

The dataset contains:

* Order date, quantity, and sales amount
* Customer & location data
* State-wise segmentation across the United States
* Daily transactional records suitable for time-series forecasting

This dataset enables trend analysis, demand forecasting, and regional sales comparison.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Retail businesses need to:

* Identify sales trends and demand cycles
* Understand regional market contributions
* Predict near-future sales accurately
* Align marketing, inventory, and operational decisions

However, raw transactional data makes it difficult to answer questions like:

* What will the sales be in the next 15 days?
* Which states generate the highest revenue?
* How do daily demand patterns change over time?

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive and forecast-driven dashboard that:

✔ Displays detailed historical sales trends
✔ Forecasts the next 15 days using predictive modeling
✔ Compares performance at the state level
✔ Supports fast and data-driven decision-making

---

### • Walkthrough of Key Visuals

#### 🔮 **15-Day Forecast – Main Trend Chart**

A time-series forecasting chart that:

* Plots historical daily sales
* Predicts upcoming 15-day performance
* Helps in planning targets, inventory, and campaigns

#### 📈 **Interactive Forecast Timeline**

A zoomable, scrollable forecast graph displaying:

* Peak and dip sales points
* Highlighted future prediction region
* Date-based filtering and drill-down capability

#### 🗺️ **Sales by State – Horizontal Bar Ranking**

A ranking view of U.S. states by their total sales:

* California leads (0.34M), followed by New York, Texas, and others
* Helps stakeholders identify top-performing regions

#### 🎛️ **Smart Controls & Slicers**

Users can:

* Filter time ranges
* Scroll through timeline
* Zoom into key performance periods

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

💡 **Inventory & Procurement Planning**
Accurate short-term forecasting helps avoid overstocking or shortages.

💡 **Regional Revenue Strategy**
Insights highlight high and low-performing states, driving budget allocation and marketing focus.

💡 **Smarter Target Setting**
Future projections allow realistic planning and performance evaluation.

💡 **Faster Executive Decision-Making**
Clean visuals and drill-through features help stakeholders identify trends instantly.
